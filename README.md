One line, at most 140 characters, that gives the judge the funniest resignation letter a person could actually send: an office fire warden quits, and the joke is that his whole career was spent waiting for a fire that never came — six drills, zero fires, and the only alarm that ever went off was his career itself.

> I resign as office fire warden, effective immediately: six drills, zero fires, and I have concluded my whole career was a false alarm.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program assembles the letter from its three beats, self-checks the kit's gates (UTF-8, 1–140 characters, one line, not whitespace-only, no control characters) and refuses to write if any gate fails, then writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and verifies it by reading it back.
